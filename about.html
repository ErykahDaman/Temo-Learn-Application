About Temo-Learn

Temo is Setswana for farming. We turn sustainable-farming knowledge into practical courses and games for Botswana's farmers, students and young innovators.

Our mission

Farming in drylands is changing fast. The central idea behind Temo-Learn is simple: teach people to grow more intelligently with limited water, extreme heat, variable rainfall and constrained resources.

Every course combines scientific foundations, practical local applications and a game that lets you experiment safely before applying a technique in the real world. Where an idea is still experimental, like electroculture, we teach it as something to test properly, not as a promise.

What we teach

Four principles

💧

Water-first farming

Efficient irrigation, rainwater capture, water reuse and responsible borehole management.

🌡️

Heat-resilient production

Shade, passive cooling, insulation and solar-powered systems.

🌱

Living soil ecosystems

Organic matter, compost, soil cover, biodiversity and nutrient cycling.

🎮

Learn by playing

Simulations, strategy puzzles, resource management and consequence-driven decisions.
